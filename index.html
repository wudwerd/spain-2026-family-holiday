The full daily plan

Every day has a gentle main idea, somewhere easy to eat and enough slack for a group of ten.

Chapter one · 3 nightsGirona

Colourful riverside streets, medieval walls and one lake-swim day.

Heathrow → Barcelona → Girona
01

Thursday 20 August 2026 · arrival day

Touch down,
slowly.

Travel day

The first night is about one thing: getting everyone from Heathrow to Girona fed, settled and ready for a proper start tomorrow.

12:30Lunch & lounge · HeathrowAfter security, the two Platinum Amex cardholders can use eligible lounge access before boarding. Check the selected lounge's guest and child rules.
15:15British Airways LHR → BCNDirect flight, 2 hr 20 min; scheduled arrival 18:35 local time.
18:35Car pickup & transferAllow about 1 hr 20 to 30 min from Barcelona Airport to Girona, plus the car handover.
21:15Dinner · GironaKeep it simple near the stay: pizza, tapas or takeaway rather than a long first-night booking.
BA direct 2 Platinum cards 1 hr 20 to 30 Girona night 1/3
Practical notes

ParkingPre-arrange a space outside Girona’s pedestrian old quarter or use the stay’s recommended car park.

Lounge planCheck the Amex lounge finder, opening hours, guest allowance and child policy. Plan an adult handover if the whole group cannot enter together.

Pack / bookCabin-bag snacks, medicines and sleepwear. Confirm late check-in and the hire-car child-seat plan.

Girona old town
02

Friday 21 August 2026 · Girona

Walls, bridges
& gelato.

City walk

A choose-your-own Girona loop: the Onyar bridges, Jewish Quarter lanes and as much of the old walls as the youngest legs want.

09:00Onyar & old townCross the riverside bridges, then wander the narrow lanes before the midday heat.
12:30Lunch · Plaça de la IndependènciaChoose a shaded terrace or assemble a market picnic for maximum flexibility.
15:00ResetReturn for a rest, pool or quiet hour; regroup for the city walls after 17:00.
19:30Dinner · old townBook a family-friendly terrace, then finish with a short golden-hour river walk.
2 to 4 km Steps optional Girona night 2/3
Practical notes

ParkingLeave the car parked. Girona’s centre works best on foot.

WalkingModerate cobbles and steps; do a short wall section and turn back whenever needed.

Pack / bookHats, water and grippy shoes. Reserve dinner for ten; cathedral entry can stay optional.

Lake Banyoles
03

Saturday 22 August 2026 · Banyoles

A freshwater
interlude.

Lake day

A gentle day-trip from Girona for waterside wandering and a supervised swim only in one of Banyoles’ designated bathing areas.

09:00Drive to BanyolesAbout 25 to 30 min each way; arrive early and choose the legal swimming area first.
10:00Lake timeSwim, hire a boat if available, or do a short out-and-back section of the flat lakeside path.
13:00Lunch · lakeside picnicBring a big picnic or choose a shaded café near the eastern shore.
18:30Dinner · GironaReturn for downtime, then an early supper and a relaxed final Girona evening.
25 to 30 min Designated swim only Girona night 3/3
Practical notes

ParkingUse signed public parking near the lake and avoid blocking residents or cycling paths.

WalkingEasy and mostly flat; the full lake circuit is about 8 km, so choose only a family-sized section.

Pack / bookSwim kit, towels, shade and picnic. Check the approved swim zones and any paid access before leaving.

Chapter two · 7 nightsCamping Palafrugell

One home base, six coves and permission to do absolutely nothing.

Girona → Camping Palafrugell
04

Sunday 23 August 2026 · Calella de Palafrugell

Settle into
the coast.

Check-in

Move to Camping Palafrugell, unpack once and celebrate the main stay with a first saltwater swim at Calella.

10:30Girona → campsiteAllow about 50 to 60 min, then check in, make beds and establish the shared snack shelf.
13:00Lunch · campsite picnicBread, fruit, cheese and easy cold food while everyone gets oriented.
16:00Calella first swimChoose Port Bo or Canadell for paddling, rock-pooling and a low-pressure beach afternoon.
19:30Dinner · campsiteBarbecue or simple pasta, followed by a walk for ice cream if energy survives.
50 to 60 min Easy first swim Camp night 1/7
Practical notes

ParkingUnload at Camping Palafrugell, then walk or use signed village parking rather than chasing seafront spaces.

WalkingEasy village paths with some slopes and steps to the beach.

Pack / bookKeep swim kit on top of the luggage. Confirm campsite arrival time; no beach booking needed.

Calella → Llafranc
05

Monday 24 August 2026 · Calella & Llafranc

Walk the
waterline.

Coastal walk

Swim at Canadell, follow the family-friendly stretch of the coastal path to Llafranc and reward the group with a long waterfront lunch.

09:30Canadell swimClaim a base, snorkel along the edge in calm conditions and rotate adults on shade duty.
11:45Coastal walkAbout 1.5 km / 35 to 45 min each way with children, steps and photo stops.
13:00Lunch · Llafranc promenadeBook one relaxed table for rice, fish or children’s favourites; split tables only if the group prefers.
19:00Dinner · campsiteLeftovers, salads and an early night after the walk.
1.5 km each way Easy to moderate Camp night 2/7
Practical notes

ParkingLeave the car at camp or use upper Calella parking; waterfront spaces fill early.

WalkingPaved but stepped, exposed in places and unsuitable for a pushchair all the way.

Pack / bookGrippy sandals, water and hats. Reserve lunch for ten and check sea conditions before snorkelling.

Tamariu
06

Tuesday 25 August 2026 · Tamariu

The snorkel
day.

Cove day

Tamariu’s compact cove is ideal for a big group: clear water, a visible beach base and optional kayaks for confident older children.

08:45Drive to TamariuAbout 15 to 20 min; arrive early enough to park and choose a clear meeting point.
09:30Swim & snorkelBuddy up, stay within the marked bathing area and keep one adult on shore.
13:00Lunch · TamariuReserve a seafront table or bring a picnic if ten damp diners feels ambitious.
18:30Dinner · campsite barbecueMake this the shared grill night; children can wind down close to home.
15 to 20 min Best snorkel Camp night 3/7
Practical notes

ParkingUse the signed upper car parks; the final walk down is steep and spaces disappear quickly.

WalkingShort but sloping. The beach is pebbly in places, so water shoes help.

Pack / bookMasks, rash vests, dry bag and reef-safe SPF. Pre-book kayaks and lunch if wanted.

Campsite & Palafrugell
07

Wednesday 26 August 2026 · slow day

Pool, pause
& potter.

Downtime

The deliberate nothing-much day: campsite pool, laundry, games and a short Palafrugell wander only when everyone is ready.

09:00Campsite morningNo alarm: swim, read, wash beach towels and give the car a day off.
12:30Lunch · at campBig salad, tortilla and fruit in the shade; save the restaurant energy for tonight.
16:30Palafrugell potterAbout 10 min by car for the centre, ice cream and the Cork Museum if the group wants culture and air-con.
19:00Dinner · PalafrugellPizza or tapas in town, then home before everyone unravels.
10 min optional Maximum downtime Camp night 4/7
Practical notes

ParkingUse a central public car park in Palafrugell and walk the compact centre.

WalkingEasy and optional; make the museum the hot-weather plan B.

Pack / bookLaundry coins or supplies, cards and sketchbooks. Check museum hours; book dinner for ten.

Cap Roig & El Golfet
08

Thursday 27 August 2026 · Cap Roig

Gardens above,
cove below.

Nature day

Balance one cultural stop with a swim: explore Cap Roig’s terraces early, then descend only if the group fancies El Golfet’s little rocky beach.

09:15Cap Roig GardensRoughly 10 to 15 min from camp; take the shady routes and let the children lead the photo hunt.
12:30Lunch · picnicEat near the gardens or return to camp before the steepest heat.
16:00El Golfet optionA small cove reached by steps; swap for Calella if seas are rough or legs are tired.
19:30Dinner · Calella or campReserve a village table for sunset, or keep dinner at base for a quieter night.
10 to 15 min Steep beach access Camp night 5/7
Practical notes

ParkingUse the garden car park; El Golfet roadside space is extremely limited.

WalkingGardens are moderate with slopes. El Golfet adds many steps and uneven surfaces.

Pack / bookClosed sandals, water and a compact picnic. Advance garden tickets and dinner are sensible.

Begur & Aiguablava
09

Friday 28 August 2026 · Begur

Blue water,
early start.

Big beach day

Aiguablava earns the early alarm: pale sand, beautiful water and good snorkelling, followed by Begur’s lanes once the beach gets busy.

08:15Drive to AiguablavaAllow 25 to 30 min on winding local roads and aim to park before the main rush.
09:00Swim & snorkelSet a bright beach marker, use buddies and explore the rocky edges only in calm water.
12:30Lunch · beach or BegurBook ahead near the cove or drive uphill for an easier family lunch in town.
18:30Dinner · campsiteSimple rice, vegetables and an early night after the busiest beach day.
25 to 30 min Arrive early Camp night 6/7
Practical notes

ParkingFollow live signage; access may be controlled when the cove is full. Never rely on roadside space.

WalkingShort from the car park but sloping. Begur’s old streets and castle are steeper.

Pack / bookShade, masks, water shoes and lots of water. Book lunch; keep a Tamariu or pool fallback.

Sant Sebastià & favourite cove
10

Saturday 29 August 2026 · last coast day

One more
sea view.

Coast finale

Take the car up to Sant Sebastià for the panorama, then let the children vote for the final swim before packing the beach kit.

09:30Sant Sebastià lookoutAbout 15 min by car; enjoy the lighthouse view without committing to the steep full walk from Llafranc.
12:30Lunch · LlafrancA final waterfront lunch, booked ahead, with time for one last paddle.
15:30Children’s choice swimReturn to Calella, Tamariu or the pool, whichever was the group favourite.
19:30Dinner · camp farewellUse up the fridge, toast the coast and pack only after the children are settled.
15 min Easy lookout Camp night 7/7
Practical notes

ParkingUse signed spaces near the lighthouse; turn back if the upper road is congested.

WalkingThe lookout is easy from the car. The path down to Llafranc is steep and optional.

Pack / bookBinoculars, hats and one dry towel each. Reserve lunch; dry snorkels and shoes before packing.

Chapter three · 3 nightsFalset & Priorat

Family time at Bigotis Del Gat, warm village evenings and one dramatic hilltop outing.

Camping Palafrugell → Falset
11

Sunday 30 August 2026 · transfer to Falset

Saltwater
to vines.

Transfer

Pack up the campsite and cross Catalonia to Falset, where the final three nights are about family, Bigotis Del Gat and a much slower table.

09:30Check outLeave Camping Palafrugell after breakfast with swimwear and snacks accessible.
10:30Drive to FalsetAllow about 2 hr 45 min to 3 hr plus one comfort stop; avoid stretching the transfer too far.
13:30Lunch · en route picnicStop halfway in shade or eat on arrival, depending on traffic and the children.
19:00Dinner · with familyA relaxed first evening in Falset and the gentlest introduction to Bigotis Del Gat.
2 hr 45 min to 3 hr Family arrival Falset night 1/3
Practical notes

ParkingConfirm the Falset parking plan privately before setting off; do not rely on narrow vineyard access for several cars.

WalkingVery light today. Vineyard ground can be dusty, steep and uneven.

Pack / bookClosed shoes, water and car snacks. Confirm campsite checkout and Falset arrival arrangements.

Bigotis Del Gat · Priorat
12

Monday 31 August 2026 · Bigotis Del Gat

A day among
the vines.

Family day

The heart of the Falset stay: an unhurried family day with Estelle’s dad at Bigotis Del Gat, shaped around heat, harvest activity and the children.

09:00Bigotis Del Gat morningWalk a short safe route together, learn what is growing and keep everyone on the agreed paths.
13:00Lunch · family tableMake this the long lunch: local bread, tomatoes, fruit and plenty of water alongside the grown-up tasting.
16:00Shade & resetQuiet games, a pool if available or downtime back in Falset during the hottest hours.
19:30Dinner · FalsetStroll into town for an easy terrace supper, or stay with family if the day is flowing.
Short uneven walk Family-first Falset night 2/3
Practical notes

ParkingFollow the family’s instructions and keep access clear for vineyard work.

WalkingUneven, dusty and potentially steep. Children stay with an adult and away from machinery.

Pack / bookClosed shoes, hats, refillable bottles and a light layer. No public booking needed; coordinate privately.

Siurana · Priorat
13

Tuesday 1 September 2026 · Siurana or Bellmunt

One last
adventure.

Hilltop day

Choose Siurana’s dramatic village and viewpoints in cooler weather, or the Bellmunt mines as a contained cultural alternative when heat or young legs win.

09:00Drive to SiuranaAbout 35 to 40 min on winding roads; park outside the village and keep children close near viewpoints.
10:00Village wanderShort lanes, church and selected viewpoints, with no cliff-edge scrambling for the group.
12:45Lunch · Siurana or CornudellaReserve a table with shade, or bring a picnic and retreat before the hottest hours.
19:00Dinner · final Falset eveningFavourite local food, family time and a calm pack for tomorrow’s flight.
35 to 40 min Cliff supervision Falset night 3/3
Practical notes

ParkingUse designated parking before Siurana and obey any access controls; roads are narrow.

WalkingModerate cobbles with unguarded drops nearby. Bellmunt, about 15 min from Falset, is the cooler plan B.

Pack / bookGrippy shoes, hats and water. Book lunch and any Bellmunt mine tour in advance.

Falset → Barcelona → Heathrow
14

Wednesday 2 September 2026 · flight home

Goodbye vines,
hello home.

Homeward

A last Falset morning, a generous airport buffer and the evening flight back to Heathrow.

09:00Morning & packingSlow breakfast, family goodbyes and a final check of passports, chargers and car-return details.
12:30Lunch · FalsetEat before the road so the airport run is not controlled by hungry children.
14:30Drive to Barcelona AirportAllow about 1 hr 45 min to 2 hr, then fuel, return the car and walk to the terminal.
17:00Dinner & lounge · Barcelona AirportThe two Platinum Amex cardholders can use eligible lounge access after the car return. Check guest and child rules before travel.
19:30British Airways BCN → LHRDirect flight 2 hr 15 min, arriving Heathrow at 20:45 local time.
1 hr 45 min to 2 hr Lounge stop BA direct LHR 20:45
Practical notes

ParkingFollow the hire-car return signs at BCN and allow time for fuel and unloading ten people.

Lounge planCheck the eligible Barcelona lounge, opening hours, guest allowance and child policy before travel. Keep snacks as a fallback.

Pack / bookPassports, medicines and devices in cabin bags. Recheck live traffic, terminal and flight status that morning.
