Ten days in Catalonia, loosely held.

Ten of us, 20 to 30 August. Three nights in Pals, then a week among the coves. Three appointments hold the shape. Everything else is weather and whim.

20 Aug · land, drive to Pals
23 Aug · down to Camping Palafrugell
30 Aug · fly home from Barcelona

Two bases. No timetable.

Each base has more around it than we could ever do. Pick by mood on the day. Some go, some stay, everyone meets back for dinner.

Chapter I

Pals & the Empordà

20 to 23 August / Camping Resort Bungalow Park

The first base sits between golden medieval villages and a huge dune-backed beach. Nothing here needs an alarm clock. The village of Pals itself is a five minute drive, best at the end of the day when the stone turns amber and the coaches have gone.

Pals old town

Golden stone, best at dusk

A hilltop knot of medieval lanes ending at the Torre de les Hores and a lookout over rice fields to the sea. Go up in the last hour of light, let the children lead the maze, finish with gelato in the square.

Drive
5 min from the bungalows
Parking
Signed car parks below the old town, walk up
Kids
Ramparts and towers to conquer, no barriers on some drops

Platja de Pals

The big sky beach

Three kilometres of dune-backed sand with room for ten of us and everyone else. Gentle shelving at the northern end, and the pine woods behind give shade when the afternoon gets serious.

Drive
10 min
Parking
Large areas behind the dunes, fills by late morning in August
Kids
Space to run, some surf on windy days

Peratallada

The prettiest village in the Empordà

Carved out of a single outcrop of stone, moat and all. Twenty minutes of lanes, then a long lunch in one of the courtyard restaurants. This is the slow morning done properly.

Drive
15 min
Parking
Field car park outside the walls, small fee
Kids
Car-free lanes, ice cream, a moat to peer into

Girona

The city option

Bridges over the Onyar, the Jewish Quarter, cathedral steps and the best gelato of the trip. Worth a day if the mood turns urban, and easily cut short if it does not.

45 min
Parking

L’Estartit & the Illes Medes

Boats over a marine reserve

Glass-bottom boats circle the protected islands just offshore, fish visible without getting wet. The harbour town behind is easy and flat, with a long family beach if the boat is enough excitement.

Drive
25 min
Parking

Kids

Estany de Banyoles

Fresh water under wooded hills

Catalonia’s largest natural lake, with designated swimming zones and rowing boats gliding past. A different kind of swim, cool and green, if anyone tires of salt.

50 to 55 min

The coves

23 to 30 August / Camping Palafrugell

Beyond the towel.

For the days when someone wants more than sand. All optional, all beautiful.

The Camí de Ronda

The golden hour walk

The old coastguard path stitches Calella to Llafranc in twenty minutes of pines, coves and turquoise glimpses. Walk it before dinner when the light is honey and the day trippers have gone.

Access
Start at Port Bo in Calella, finish on the Llafranc promenade
Parking

Kids
Paved but stepped, not for pushchairs

Far de Sant Sebastià

The lighthouse view

Above Llafranc, an 18th century lighthouse, a watchtower and the whole coastline laid out below. There is a hotel terrace for a slow drink while the children inspect the tower.

The runner’s pages.

Three routes from the gate of Camping Palafrugell, one per training intent. Distances are map-measured; treat them as ±5% and calibrate on the first run. August sunrise is around 07:05.

01

The lighthouse out-and-back

Hill quality on the Camí de Ronda
Distance
10.5 km
Gain
≈300 m
Surface
60% path
Start
06:45

Out of the gate, down the Calella road, and onto the Camí de Ronda through Port Bo to Llafranc. The business end is the climb to the Far de Sant Sebastià: 1.1 km at 8% average with two pitches near 12%. Touch the lighthouse balustrade, retrace. The granite steps polish smooth near the coves, so shorten stride on the descents; after 09:00 the path is single-file with walkers.

02

The plain tempo loop

Flat and fast through Llofriu and Mont-ras
Distance
11.2 km
Gain
≈70 m
Surface
90% lane
Start
07:15

The inland side of Palafrugell is a quiet grid of farm lanes across the plain: out through the town’s north-west edge, Llofriu, Mont-ras, home. Kilometres 2 to 9 are effectively dead flat and traffic-free, which makes this the uninterrupted tempo block of the week. The lanes are unlit and unsigned, preload the route; corners look identical between the fields.

03

The Gavarres long run

Cork-oak forest tracks toward Fitor
Distance
20.5 km
Gain
≈520 m
Surface
70% track
Start
06:30

Through Llofriu and up into the Gavarres: wide forest track under cork oaks nearly the whole way, climbing at a steady 4 to 6% for five kilometres to the col by the Fitor chapel, then rolling home. Run the climb on effort, not pace. The canopy holds shade until about 11:00, and the surface is kind, this is the one to bank the Sunday volume on.
